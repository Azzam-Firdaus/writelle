Privacy Policy — Writelle

Version 1.0.0 • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Writelle ("Application", "We", "Our"). Writelle is a contemplative literature haven and pseudonymous writing sanctuary dedicated to mindful slow reading, honest expression, and user privacy. We deeply respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal data you entrust to us.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Email address and encrypted password generated upon account registration.
  • Pseudonymous Profile Data: Pen name (display name), optional biography quotes, and optional avatar images uploaded by the user.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Literary tales, reflective writings, category tags, and comments you publish or draft.
  • Community Interactions: Story likes, bookmarks, and author follow relationships (readers and following counts).
  • Anonymity Preferences: Pure Anonymity publication mode to conceal author identity on select tales.

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information directly when you register, compose stories, or interact within the application. If you sign in via Google Identity, we receive only your verified email address and basic profile identifier to authenticate your account.

4. Purpose and Use of Data

  • To provide core services: account authentication and real-time contemplative story feeds.
  • To uphold community safety, enforce our Terms of Service, and moderate abusive or violating content.
  • To protect author identities when tales are published in anonymous mode.
  • We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or commercial third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

All data is securely stored within Supabase managed PostgreSQL infrastructure utilizing industry-standard TLS 1.2/1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Database access is strictly governed by granular PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Supabase Inc.: Backend-as-a-Service provider handling encrypted database storage, authentication, and media assets.
  • Google Identity (Optional): Employed solely when you explicitly choose to authenticate via Google Sign-In.

7. Local Device Storage and Sessions

The mobile application utilizes secure local device storage (SharedPreferences) to cache your authentication session token, reading theme preferences, and locally blocked author lists. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or invasive advertising SDKs.

8. Data Retention Schedule

Stories deleted by curators or users are retained in a soft-deleted quarantine state for a grace window of 14 calendar days, after which they are permanently and irreversibly purged from our database. Active profile and story data are retained for as long as your account remains active.

9. Account & Data Deletion Rights

In full compliance with Google Play Store policies and international data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, CalOPPA), you have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open Profile > Tap the gear/settings icon > Select "Delete Sanctuary Account". Your profile, published tales, comments, and authentication records are permanently erased immediately.
  • Public Web Deletion Portal: If you no longer have access to the app, visit our Public Account Deletion Page or submit a direct email request to our developer address below.

10. Children's Privacy

Writelle is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
