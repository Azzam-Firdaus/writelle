Account & Personal Data Deletion Request

Google Play Store User Data Policy Compliance

Users of the Writelle mobile application retain full autonomy to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data stored on our servers at any time.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant & Automated)

If you currently have access to the Writelle application on your device:

  1. Open Writelle and ensure you are logged into your account.
  2. Navigate to the Profile sanctuary tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the settings / gear icon in the top right corner.
  4. Select "Delete Sanctuary Account" highlighted in red.
  5. Confirm the irreversible deletion prompt.

This immediately and irreversibly purges your authentication record, pen name profile, and all published tales.

Method 2: Web & Email Request (If Application is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the application, lost access to your device, or cannot log in, submit a deletion request to our developer team:

Send an email to mazzamfirdaus06@gmail.com with the subject line Writelle Account Deletion Request from the email address registered with your Writelle account.

Submit Email Deletion Request

What Data is Permanently Erased?

  • Authentication credentials (User UUID and registered email in Supabase Auth).
  • Public profile attributes (Pen name, personal biography, and uploaded avatar photo).
  • All published stories, drafts, reflections, and category associations.
  • User interactions including comments, likes, bookmarks, and reader/following associations.

Data Retention Exception

Anonymized server security transaction logs (devoid of personal identifiers) may be retained for up to 30 calendar days strictly for regulatory audit compliance and fraud prevention prior to automatic purging.
